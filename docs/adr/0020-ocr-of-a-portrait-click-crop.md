# ADR-0020: OCR of a portrait Click crop is rotated 90° first, then read at 0°/180°

- Date: 2026-11-03
- Status: Accepted
- Source: DECISIONS.md row 20 (migrated)

## Decision

OCR of a portrait Click crop is rotated 90° first, then read at 0°/180°

## Context and reason

Report reads 0°/180° only; names run along the long side, so vertical Clicks were never read

## Evidence

synthetic contact sheet (Heart Rate at 90°)
