# ADR-0028: Calibrate anchors from the photo as stage 1 reads it, in addition to the rectified board

- Date: 2026-11-03
- Status: Accepted
- Source: DECISIONS.md row 28 (migrated)

## Decision

Calibrate anchors from the photo as stage 1 reads it, in addition to the rectified board

## Context and reason

Rectified-only anchors matched 3–5 labels per real photo (engine glues/splits text differently at another scale); with photo reads 8 and 13

## Evidence

tests/real, anchors 47 → 73
