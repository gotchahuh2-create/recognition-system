# ADR-0045: Labels alone may name a layout only with net evidence ≥ 6 and ≥ 1.5× the runner-up

- Date: 2026-11-04
- Status: Accepted
- Source: DECISIONS.md row 45 (migrated)

## Decision

Labels alone may name a layout only with net evidence ≥ 6 and ≥ 1.5× the runner-up; a few common labels never do

## Context and reason

Without a title read, 4 common SPI/I2C labels must not pick one of 11 mapped layouts
