# ADR-0040: Frame test bridges gaps (closing 3 % of size) and strips attachments (opening 6 %) instead

- Date: 2026-11-04
- Status: Accepted
- Source: DECISIONS.md row 40 (migrated)

## Decision

Frame test bridges gaps (closing 3 % of size) and strips attachments (opening 6 %) instead of using contour hierarchy

## Context and reason

Board lines and label rows touching the frame, or a 1–2 px break in it, made the frame an open / irregular contour: 2 of 12 synthetic scenes and 1 real photo were missed

## Evidence

synthetic set 12/12 located after the change
