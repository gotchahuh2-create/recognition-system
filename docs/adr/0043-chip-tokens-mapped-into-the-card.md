# ADR-0043: Chip tokens mapped into the card frame axis-preserving (scaled into the chip's box), not t

- Date: 2026-11-04
- Status: Accepted
- Source: DECISIONS.md row 43 (migrated)

## Decision

Chip tokens mapped into the card frame axis-preserving (scaled into the chip's box), not through the chip's 45° rotation

## Context and reason

Rotated mapping made stacked marking lines diagonal; line grouping glued them in the wrong order ('RET6STM32F411GH20K' → F413RH 0.89, a near-false accept)
